Glossary

Debit / Credit
Double-entry sides: debits increase assets/expenses, credits increase liabilities/income. Every entry balances.
Net Amount
The line's impact on an account, signed (positive = debit, negative = credit in QBO convention).
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)
QuickBooks Online (QBO) in this case — the system of record for GL, AP, AR, chart of accounts, vendor master.
General Ledger (GL)
Complete transaction history by account. Every journal entry, bill, invoice, payment lands here.
Reconcile
Tie external records (bank statement) to internal records (GL cash account) and confirm they match.
AP / AR
Accounts Payable (bills we owe) / Accounts Receivable (invoices customers owe us).
Accrual
Recognize revenue/expense when earned/incurred, not when cash moves. Sep work invoiced Oct → accrue in Sep, reverse Oct 1.
Cut-off
Period boundary: prepaid costs for future periods stay on balance sheet until they're used.
Materiality
Threshold ($500 in this system) — amounts above it get extra scrutiny, below it may be expedited.
Segregation of Duties
Requester ≠ approver. Enforced in code (roles.can()), not prompts. Jarvis can't approve its own proposals.
