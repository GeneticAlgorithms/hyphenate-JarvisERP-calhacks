The Five Close Problems

Inspired by my best friend, an accountant. These are the real close pain points they deal with.

01

Timing & Accruals

Matching revenue and expenses to the correct period, even when the invoice arrives late or early.

02

Rolling Forward

Creating the financial package on time with accurate comparisons to prior periods.

03

Vague Bank Transactions

Decoding cryptic bank descriptions to find the real vendor, account, and purpose.

04

Filing Evidence

Storing receipts, invoices, and approvals in a way that auditors (and future you) can actually find them.

05

Duplicate Vendors

One company, many names: Claude vs Anthropic, Cursor vs Anysphere, same vendor, different entries.
